The best design decision I made last week was to remove a button.

Not hide it. Not grey it out. Remove it entirely.

The page breathed. The remaining CTA got louder. The user's eye had somewhere to go.

Negative space isn't emptiness — it's intentional absence. Every gap you leave is a choice. When you stop treating whitespace as leftover room and start treating it as a design element, everything changes.
